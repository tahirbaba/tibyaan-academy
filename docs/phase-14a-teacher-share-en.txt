Title:
Teacher share

Body:
For every student you teach, you receive 75% of that student's monthly fee.
The remaining 25% is Tibyaan Academy's platform share, which covers hosting,
class links, recordings, student support and payment handling.

Where it shows:
- Teacher signup, before the form (when the "teacher" role is selected).
- Teacher dashboard, on the revenue/earnings screen.
